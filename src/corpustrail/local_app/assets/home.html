Welcome to CorpusTrail

CorpusTrail helps researchers find, organize and evaluate scientific publications. Build a collection around your research topic, keep track of available evidence, and understand how publications became part of your corpus.

Free, open source and local. Your projects live on your computer. CorpusTrail has no subscription, tracking or mandatory cloud service. You control each external search or transfer; external services may have their own costs and terms.

Keep the research trail

See where a publication came from, what evidence was available and how a decision was made. A clear trail helps you revisit decisions and explain or reproduce your work.

Missing evidence does not mean a publication is out of scope. Only explicitly authorized decisions change corpus membership.

ASReview — optional
Screen that collection for a specific systematic or scoping review question.

Broad-topic membership and question-specific eligibility are different decisions. Prioritization only orders review; it never automatically excludes publications or stops review.

Optional model assistance — with clear limits

Manual review works without a model. Generic assessment plans, stored results and audit workflows are implemented. Real membership-assessment engines are not installed or validated by default. The separate experimental structured-Knowledge adapter supports explicit configuration and synthetic connection tests; connecting a model does not establish scientific validity or authorize evidence transfer.

RIS bibliography import/export, including available abstracts, is implemented. Other import formats, automatic query generation, direct institutional-database connections, native installers and phone support remain separately scoped future work.

This application runs locally. Opening Home does not contact external services or change your research projects. Searches and retrieval require explicit actions; no model is required.
